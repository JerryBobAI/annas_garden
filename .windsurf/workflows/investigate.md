---
description: Systematic debugging with root cause investigation
---

Use the `skill` tool to invoke the `investigate` skill from gstack.

Four phases:
1. **Investigate** — gather evidence, read logs, reproduce the issue
2. **Analyze** — identify patterns and narrow down the cause
3. **Hypothesize** — form and test hypotheses about root cause
4. **Implement** — fix the root cause (never the symptoms)

Iron Law: no fixes without confirmed root cause.
