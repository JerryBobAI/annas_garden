description: YC Office Hours — brainstorm ideas, validate product concepts
---

Use the `skill` tool to invoke the `office-hours` skill from gstack.

Two modes:
1. **Startup mode** — six forcing questions: demand reality, status quo, desperate specificity, narrowest wedge, observation, future-fit
2. **Builder mode** — design thinking brainstorming for side projects, hackathons, learning, open source

Saves a design doc at the end.
